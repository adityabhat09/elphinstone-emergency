If You Notice a Hazard

Alert the nearest Floor Warden and activate the closest manual fire alarm call point. Command flow: Floor Warden → DM Coordinator → Principal & Liaison Officer → external agencies (Police/Fire/Ambulance). See the full Disaster Management Plan report for details.

Transparency note: no fire safety audit has ever been conducted for this building. This page is a student research prototype, not a substitute for one.

Evacuation Map

Routes below are proposed based on floor-plan review, not an approved fire-exit plan.

Ground Floor

Primary: nearest corridor → Lobby stair-block → Main Entry (M.G. Road). Alternate: North Side Entrance (near Pump Room).

First Floor

Highest routine occupancy (Library, Admin/Exam section). Central Stair Block → down to Ground Floor Lobby. Alternate: Economics Dept. stairwell (north side). Do not cut through the courtyard.

Second Floor

Highest-hazard floor (Chemistry & Research labs, Auditorium). Move away from lab benches first, then Central Stair Block → down through First Floor → Ground Floor Lobby.

Third Floor

Rooms 301–303, gymkhana/storage. No alternate stairwell — use the central stair block, descending via Second → First → Ground Floor Lobby.

Fourth Floor (Terrace)

Rotary Dept. office + open terrace. Lowest occupancy of all floors. The terrace is not an exit or refuge point — always move down via the stair block.

Proposed Assembly Point: Open compound outside the Main Entry gate on Mahatma Gandhi Road (clear of the building façade and overhead cornices).

Do's

  • Stay calm, stop work, and move toward the nearest marked exit immediately.
  • Use stairs only — never the lift — during a fire or earthquake.
  • If you smell smoke, stay low and cover your nose and mouth with cloth.
  • During an earthquake, take cover under a sturdy desk/table until shaking stops, then evacuate.
  • Assist people with disabilities, injuries, or difficulty moving.
  • Report to the assembly point and wait for a headcount/all-clear.

Don'ts

  • Don't use the elevator during a fire, earthquake, or evacuation.
  • Don't re-enter the building until officially cleared.
  • Don't stop to collect bags or belongings.
  • Don't push or run on staircases — the wooden/heritage stairs can be narrow and slippery.
  • Don't stand near glass windows, tall bookshelves, or leaning/unfixed frames during shaking.
  • Don't block corridors or stairwells by gathering there.

Emergency Procedure

  • Recognize the alarm/announcement or hazard (fire, smoke, shaking, smell of gas/chemicals).
  • Alert others nearby and activate the nearest fire alarm call point if safe to do so.
  • Evacuate via the nearest proposed route above — do not use the lift.
  • Proceed directly to the assembly point outside the Main Entry.
  • Report your presence to a class representative / staff member for headcount.
  • Call 101 (Fire) or 108 (Ambulance) if not already summoned, and inform college security.
  • Wait for an official all-clear before re-entering.
